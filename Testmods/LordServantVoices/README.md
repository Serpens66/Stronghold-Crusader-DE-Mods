# Lord Servant Voices

In Vanilla, the Rat, the Snake, the Pig and the Wolf do not speak many of their diplomacy messages themselves. A generic Servant who sounds the same for all four of them speaks instead, for example when they request or send goods, refuse help or announce an attack.

With this mod, each of these four Lords has his own Servant with his own voice for these messages. The message text shows what this Servant says; the video stays the same as in Vanilla.

- The voices exist in English and German and follow the game's speech language. With any other speech language, the Vanilla Servant and his text remain.
- The text follows the game language like in Vanilla. If the game language is neither English nor German, the Vanilla text remains.
- Messages without a recording of their own keep the Vanilla Servant: the Rat asking for help, the Snake refusing to attack, the Pig announcing a siege, sending troops or agreeing to attack, the Wolf announcing a siege, one of the Wolf's victory messages, and the Christmas greeting of all four.
- This is a local mod: other players do not need it in multiplayer.
- Requires the Script Extender and APIShared.
