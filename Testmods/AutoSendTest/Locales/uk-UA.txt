ReserveHelp=Keep this amount of the selected good for myself. Only the surplus may be sent automatically. All my deliveries of this good share this reserve.
MinimumHelp=Priority stock for this ally. At each check, allies below their minimum receive supplies first. Remaining surplus is then shared up to each ally's maximum. The minimum does not need to be reached before deliveries start. Raising it above the maximum raises the maximum too.
MaximumHelp=Stop automatic deliveries while this ally has this amount of the selected good. Resume at a later check if their stock falls below it. The minimum sets priority; the maximum sets how far to supply them. Zero switches this rule off. Lowering the maximum below the minimum lowers the minimum too. Their own production and gifts from others can raise their stock above this limit.
IntervalHelp=How often to check whether this good should be sent to this ally: 1 to 300 game seconds, default 30. 1 second = 40 ticks. No deliveries are made while the game is paused; higher game speed shortens the wait in real time. Starting automatic deliveries or changing the interval starts a full waiting period.
