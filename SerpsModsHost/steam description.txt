[h1]Serps Mods – Fully Customizable Collection[/h1]

A fully customizable collection of my mods for [b]Stronghold Crusader: Definitive Edition[/b].

Every included mod—and almost every individual feature—can be enabled, disabled, and configured through the in-game [b]Mod Settings[/b]. Presets allow you to quickly switch between different configurations.

In multiplayer, gameplay-related settings are controlled by the host and synchronized with all clients. Personal interface and control settings remain local.

[h1]Included Mods and Features[/h1]

[h2]BugfixesAndQoL[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fixes synchronized movement for mixed troop groups, making every unit move at the speed of the slowest unit.
[*]Fixes plague popularity penalties and reliably removes them after the plague has been eliminated.
[*]Prevents treated plague clouds from becoming active again.
[*]Fixes apothecaries getting stuck when leaving their buildings.
[*]Improves apothecary target reservation so multiple apothecaries do not treat the same plague area.
[*]Allows rally points to be placed anywhere.
[*]Remembers each AI lord’s selected AIV list, AIV rotation, and custom AIC settings across singleplayer and multiplayer lobbies.
[*]Fixes incorrectly tripled starting gold in custom Crusader Trails.
[*]Provides a freely customizable market goods order, including an option to restore the classic HD order.
[*]Optionally applies the configured goods order to the detailed market view.
[*]Makes the camera follow the cursor directly while dragging on the minimap.
[*]Pressing the market hotkey while the market is already open returns to the main trading menu.
[*]Fixes automatic selling when the sell threshold is greater than zero.
[*]Fixes the demolition cursor near enemy units.
[*]Allows the minimap to be used while placing a building.
[*]Allows camera movement while Ctrl or Alt is held.
[/list]

[h2]BuildingCosts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Customize the construction costs of every building type.
[*]Wood, stone, iron, pitch, and gold costs can be configured separately.
[*]Costs can remain unchanged, be reduced, increased, or removed completely.
[*]Modified construction costs apply to both human and AI players.
[/list]

[h2]BuildingLimit[/h2]
[list]
[*]Individual building limits for human players.
[*]Set a separate maximum for every building type.
[*]Any building type can remain completely unlimited.
[*]Related variants such as gardens, statues, shrines, and ponds are counted together.
[/list]

[h2]ExtraFeatures[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hold Ctrl while trading to buy or sell exactly one unit of a good.
[*]Newly recruited units run to their rally points at their own maximum movement speed.
[*]Swordsmen can mount horses, while mounted knights can dismount.
[*]Horses can optionally become available immediately after dismounting.
[*]Move a selected quarry’s linked stone pile clockwise to the next valid position.
[*]Churches can receive two priests and cathedrals three priests.
[*]Customize the maximum number of peasants waiting at the campfire.
[*]Pause a single production building independently from other buildings of the same type.
[*]Customize wood, stone, iron, pitch, and gold refunds when demolishing buildings.
[*]Preserve the contents of granaries, stockpiles, and armories when they are demolished.
[*]Apply separate goods-production multipliers to human and AI players.
[*]Convert additional produced goods into gold based on their selling value.
[*]Set global multipliers for market buying and selling prices.
[*]Configure individual buying and selling multipliers for every market good.
[*]Optionally make the AI use the modified market prices for its decisions, purchases, and sales.
[*]Prevent the AI from pausing its production buildings.
[*]Prevent the AI’s emergency demolition of useful buildings to recover resources.
[*]Prevent the AI from directly deleting active hovels.
[*]Customize the active duration of plague clouds.
[*]Customize how far apothecaries search for plague clouds.
[/list]

[i]Note: Knight mounting and dismounting, quarry pile relocation, and pausing individual buildings are not currently available in multiplayer.[/i]

[h2]RandomEvents[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds freely configurable positive and negative random events.
[*]Customize the event interval and event-specific cooldown.
[*]Set an individual probability for every event.
[*]Configure the minimum and maximum strength of each event type.
[*]Positive events: Fair, Marriage, and Bard.
[*]Negative events: Plague, Wheat Infestation, Hops Beetles, Apple Blight, Tree Blight, Rabbits, Lion Attack, Bandits, Mad Cows, Archers, Granary Theft, and Fire.
[*]Bandit and archer attacks can scale with the elapsed game time.
[*]The strength or size of plagues, lion attacks, thefts, and fires can be configured separately.
[/list]

[i]Note: Random Events is currently disabled completely in network games.[/i]

[h2]CastlePlanner[/h2]
[list]
[*]Load custom AIVJSON castles as visible local blueprints.
[*]Toggle the blueprint while constructing your castle.
[*]Assign any supported keyboard key or mouse button as the blueprint hotkey.
[*]Customize blueprint icon size and opacity.
[*]Spawn the selected AIVJSON castle directly in a new singleplayer game.
[*]Castle selection and blueprints remain completely local in multiplayer and do not affect other players.
[/list]

[h2]StartConditions[/h2]
[list]
[*]Set or add starting gold separately for human and AI players.
[*]Supports different gold values for regular Crusader and Deathmatch games.
[*]Configure every starting good individually for human and AI players.
[*]Set separate starting goods for regular Crusader and Deathmatch games.
[*]Remove, preserve, or multiply the official starting armies separately for human and AI players.
[*]Add custom amounts of every available unit type as additional starting troops.
[/list]

[h2]UnitCosts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Modify the native gold cost of every recruitable unit (modified base costs apply to both human and AI players)
[*]Add extra gold and material costs for human players.
[*]Missing materials are shown directly when recruitment is attempted.
[/list]

[h2]UnitLimit[/h2]
[list]
[*]Individual unit limits for human players to finally balance the game.
[*]Eg. only up to 5 catapults/ballistas or healers to not make the game too easy.
[*]Set a separate maximum for every unit type.
[/list]

[h1]Configuration and Presets[/h1]
[list]
[*]Every included mod has its own in-game Mod Settings page.
[*]Mods and individual features can be enabled or disabled independently.
[*]Multiple local presets allow you to create different rulesets, such as Vanilla+, massive armies, or heavily modified economies.
[*]In multiplayer, the host controls all gameplay-related settings.
[*]Local client options, such as interface, controls, and blueprints, can be configured independently by each player.
[*]An integrated diagnostics page verifies that all parts of the collection were loaded and registered correctly.
[/list]
