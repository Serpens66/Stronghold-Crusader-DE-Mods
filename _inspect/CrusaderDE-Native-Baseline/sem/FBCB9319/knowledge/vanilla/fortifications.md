## Gatehouse footprint origin (read first)

This fact is referenced by the working rules and is easy to get wrong:

- **Vanilla measures from the start tile corner `Start × 8`.** The gatehouse loop scales the footprint begin tile by eight on X and Y (`0xB7B78`, `0xB7B8C`) and compares against unit fine world X/Y. Neither the footprint size nor the access tile takes part in Vanilla's origin. `[confirmed, disassembly]` ([GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md](../GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md#2026-11-10-gatehouse-footprint-origin-and-closing-cadence))
- **Units stand at tile centers `tile × 8 + 4`.** Editor placement `0x118CA0` scales both tile axes by eight (`0x118D7E`, `0x118D88`) and calls `0x17FEF0` (`0x118DB1`); the spawn adds four (`0x17FF60`, `0x17FF95`) and writes fine X/Y at `0x17FFDD`/`0x17FFA2`, the same fields the gatehouse loop reads at `0xB7B70`/`0xB7B9F`. `[confirmed, disassembly]` ([GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md](../GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md))
- **Square gate center is `Start × 8 + gridSize × 4`.** Tile `t` spans `t*8..(t+1)*8`. Example: begin 100, size 7 → Vanilla origin 800, true center 828 (tile 103 centered). `begin*8 + (size-1)*4` = 824 is half a tile off. Grid size is record `+0xF8` (`r_OccupyTileGridSize`); no occupied-tile list or access coordinates are needed. `[confirmed, static arithmetic on confirmed fields]` ([GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md](../GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md))
- Closing test: `max(|8*X - unitX|, |8*Y - unitY|) < 200`, or `< 140` with the role flag from `0xB7AB2` (strict). A unit at distance ≥ 200 can never close the gate. `[confirmed, static]` ([GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md](../GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md), [GATE_BRIDGE_AUTOMATION.md](../GATE_BRIDGE_AUTOMATION.md))

## Overview

Buildings live in the building manager (RVA `0x64CCBB0`, one-based sentinel-relative slots of stride `0x32C`, public `GameBuilding` view at slot `+0x5C`). Map tiles live in the tile manager (RVA `0x405EDB0`, 320800 packed tiles, layers listed below). Human construction enters through managed `EngineInterface.PlaceMapperItem` → export `DLL_MapAction` `0x85BD0` → `0x8AAF0`, which forwards buildings to `0x90CD0`, walls to `0x94120` and demolition (mapper 39) to `0x90830`. Execution happens through Chores: 28 (building, handler `0x12090` → `BuildStructure` `0x6D580`), 25 (wall line, `0x11B50` → `0x6F230`), 29/41 (typed and wall removal), 21 (moat/pitch brush), 43 (type sleep), 68 (repair), 75 (Undo). AI castles go through the AIV executor `0x51790` → `0x6CB20` (walls) or `0x6D580`. Validation `0x77E60` (buildings) and `0x7A3B0` (walls) serves both preview and execution. Removal is two-phase: `0xC4290` marks state 3, the building dispatcher `0xC60F0` later runs terminal cleanup `0xB8310`. Gatehouses run `0xB73D0` once per building pass for capture and automatic closing; drawbridges are separate buildings coupled through perimeter adjacency `0xB9330`. Wall/crenel shape is a deferred render-maintenance product of `0x65830` that also writes the gameplay-relevant crenel high bit.

## Open questions and contradictions

- Gate cadence: [GATE_BRIDGE_AUTOMATION.md](../GATE_BRIDGE_AUTOMATION.md) says a 50-tick enemy scan; the first part of [GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md](../GATEHOUSE_QUERY_PASS.md) says the unit loop runs at `tick % 10 == (phase+5) % 10`. Its 2026-11-10 correction resolves this: modulo 10 gates capture, the enemy scan is `tick % 50 == phase % 20`.
- Enemy list bases differ: `0x38C19E8 + p*20000` / `0x38ED908 + p*40000` (hostile list of `0x181340`) versus `0x3809EBB + p*0x4E20` / `0x3835DF3 + p*0x9C40` (gate loop). Strides agree; whether these are the same list in another base convention or two lists is not established.
- `0x65830` frequency: "once per simulation block" ([PITCH_STAIRS_HEIGHT.md](../PITCH_STAIRS_HEIGHT.md), call `0xCE0E1`) versus every `DLL_RunTick` frame incl. frames without a simulation tick (later correction in [CRENELATED_WALLS.md](../CRENELATED_WALLS.md)). Treat the later statement as current.
- Building goods/checksum offsets in [BUILDING_SLEEP_SELECTION.md](../BUILDING_SLEEP_SELECTION.md) (`0x120..`, `0xD0..0x32B`) do not state their base. As slot offsets the stone/iron words would overlap the slot state at `+0x12C`; as record offsets the range would exceed the record. Re-verify before use.
- Corrected earlier claims (do not re-derive): Chore 43 carries a two-byte type, not a four-byte ID; Chore 25 is 11 consumed bytes, not six Int32; dug-moat removal is one operation, not four steps; Keep direction is `+0x92`, not `r_SpriteVariationIndex`; pre-built AI castles keep all walls (the "39 of 117" loss was a loop bound error); +0xFA2980 is the damage layer; MiscDisplay `0x2000` on pitch is local visibility, not a footprint-edge mark; farm coverage is not "2,2,1"; SE comments swap delete/bulldoze names (`c_game_building_delete_hook_impl` → `0xC4290` mark, `c_game_building_bulldoze_hook_impl` → `0xB8310` terminal).
- Open: full Chore 21 PlannedMoat cancellation contract; meaning of `0x90CD0`/`0x1D160` calls from `0x8AAF0`/`0xCDE60`; logic bit 7 (`0x6903D`) and bit 15 (`0x7C1FF`/`0x7C320`) writes in the render pass; which projectile types reach `0x7DF60` wall branch; scenario of `0xED2E0`; dispatch behind `0x12E400`; AI siege-target choice; damage `<= 20` dock threshold; ladder into a later-high crenel (probable edge-less); "doubled" look of wall-less diagonal crenel rows; whether low special sprites look high; AI 0-cost walls not placed (once, candidate); register-only compensation forms not covered by the pitch scan; save layer table `0x336E38` recompute (probable).
