# AI resource reserve audit

Native target: installed `CrusaderDE.dll`, SHA-256 `FBCB93195FC7EFCA9BDAC5204852EFDD76F9818F59A6711750D77C9CEF2831E2`.

The AI update path at RVA `0x57330` reaches the market and production logic at `0x2AE40`. The stock probe at `0x2AA20` waits until wood planks or iron have been empty for more than 36 AI rounds, then queues five wood planks or two iron ingots. The pending-purchase processor at `0x3EC90` checks market access and prices, handles at most one successful purchase per AI round, and clears that queue entry after success. The transaction path at `0x29650` calls `0xB58B0`; the price helper is `0xCEB10`. No equivalent fixed Ale queue writer has been established by this audit. Generic recruitment queue writers at `0x40230`, `0x40430`, and `0x40740` are another reason to preserve nonzero pending amounts.

Managed `FRONT_Multiplayer.StartSkirmishGame` creates `HUD_IngameMenu.RestartSkirmishMapInfo`, imports the eight `MPAIVInfo` selections, stores it on `MainViewModel.Instance.HUDIngameMenu.restartSkirmishMapInfo`, and then calls `EngineInterface.initMultiplayerGame`. For a selected custom Lord it later passes `AIVs[playerId-1].lordConfig.lordData` to `EngineInterface.setCustomLordConfig`. `MPAIVInfo` exposes `builtInLord` and `lordConfig`; `CustomLordConfig` carries name and checksum. Its binary decode does not restore a local path, so saved selections must be mapped to an installed local configuration by name and checksum and must fail closed when absent or ambiguous. The existing ExtendedData host snapshot remains authoritative in real multiplayer, and its pending Trail snapshot remains authoritative for custom Trails.

The Script Extender exposes `GameAIManagerAPI.GetGoodFromPendingPurchases` and `SetGoodToPendingPurchases` on the native pending array. `GameTimeManagerAPI.OnTick` is the long-lived simulation-tick event. These public APIs permit an additive testmod that queues a bounded deficit while leaving actual purchasing, pricing, and gold deduction with Vanilla. Ale purchase behavior and survival of the new runtime past startup cleanup require in-game evidence.
