## Resource and cleanup contracts [Vanilla facts]

The lord-death path described in DEFEAT_LOOT.md is unchanged. Reward setup starts
at RVA `0x15C48D`, after the one-shot marker, winner-zero and special-flag guards.
At `0x15C4EA`, RBP is the credited one-based winner, RBX/R14 are the one-based
defeated player, R11 is defeatedId*0x160F and R12D is the clamped gold reward.
The first displaced instructions compute winner indices R10/R13, ending at
`0x15C4F8`; the first award write is at `0x15C4FC`.

The raw player-zero resources begin at image+`0x379ADD0`. Add playerId*`0x583C`;
the Extender's first resource record is manager+`0x1343FC`, with a zero-based
array, so its record for playerId uses `(playerId-1)`. These formulas agree.
The total-goods table begins at record+`0x4D0`, incoming goods at +`0x46C`, and
signed current gold at +`0x50C`. Native source reads and additions are Int32.
The twenty indices in image+`0x3327B0` are
`21,22,24,18,6,20,17,7,8,23,9,14,19,3,4,10,11,12,13,2`.
They exclude gold, logs, hides and flour. The goods-notification write is the
record+`0x3C` integer set to 2, only after positive total goods credit.

Cleanup begins at `0x15C980` and requires RCX=image+`0x366C210`, R11, RBX and
R12D. Its zero writes clear the defeated player's **incoming table**, from
`0x46C` through `0x4CC` (25 entries including gold), not the total-goods source
table. This sharpens the older audit's generic loser-goods-cleanup description.
At `0x15C997`, R12D is added to image+`0x087EED98`+winnerId*4. The following mode
guards affect notification visibility; they do not undo the credited reward.
The type-8 victory message precedes the type-1 gold message. The follow-up at
`0x15CA9B` remains reachable when only the gold message is omitted.

## Statistic-address correction [2026-10-27 corrective audit]

The canonical DLL hash above was verified again. The actual instructions are
`4C 8D 2D 91 79 41 08` at `0x15C988` (LEA R13,[RIP+0x08417991]) and
`45 01 A4 AD 78 AA 27 00` at `0x15C997` (ADD [R13+RBP*4+0x27AA78],R12D).
Thus `0x15C98F + 0x08417991 + 0x0027AA78 = 0x087EED98` (VA `0x1887EED98`).
SizeOfImage is `0x08903000`; `.data` begins at `0x00240000` with virtual size
`0x086AD624` and writable initialized-data characteristics. Slots zero through
eight fit in this section and image. The earlier `0x887EED98` exceeds the image;
it was a transcription error, not an alternative native layout. Original raw
evidence in `.work/a/kill-reward/001` remains historical and unmodified.

Production now derives the RVA from both validated operands, validates the full
player range and writable non-executable data section before publishing hooks,
and verifies the live anchors before commit. Native regressions reject the old
bad value and exercise the production pointer/credit path inside the actual
mapped DLL layout, rather than only separate local struct buffers.

## Gold message capacity [Vanilla facts]

The block `0x15CA40..0x15CA6F` splits gold into remainder and quotient in base
30000. `FUN_18001c8d0` (RVA `0x1C8D0`) writes each of its five payload fields
as 16-bit words into a ten-entry ring. The managed `FatControler` type-1 consumer
reconstructs `chat_store_data[n,3] + chat_store_data[n,4]*30000`. Consequently an
arbitrary Int32 gold reward cannot safely use one original message. Chunking at
900,000,000 gives quotient <=30000 and remainder <=29999, safely representable
even for signed interpretation. At most three chunks represent Int32.MaxValue.
This does not enlarge or change the existing ten-entry message ring.

## ExtraFeatures integration [mod integration]

The existing permanent 14-byte reward and 15-byte message spans remain the only
two hooks. Configured roles compute 64-bit percentages and bounded credit before
any write, add gold directly and goods into incoming entries, then set RCX and
R12D to the resource base and actual award and branch to the original cleanup.
Its statistic write and victory/follow-up paths remain Vanilla. The message
stub suppresses zero and splits positive awards into safe native messages;
Win64 nonvolatile winner/base/remaining registers survive each message call.
Unconfigured roles keep the complete original award path.

The callback validates Extender resource pointers against the audited formulas;
record size, public field offsets and twenty enum identities are checked before
hook publication. New addresses remain native-hash-bound. Native fixtures execute
the production transfer and generated message code; they do not establish actual
Unity/Noesis rendering, game lifecycle or genuine multiplayer acceptance.
