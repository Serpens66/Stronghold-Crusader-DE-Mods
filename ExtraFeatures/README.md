# ExtraFeatures

- Recruited units "run" to rally points at their max speed
- Monks always run
- Swordsmen can mount horses, while mounted knights can dismount
- Move a quarry’s linked stone pile to another valid position
- Churches and cathedrals receive more priests
- Customize max number of peasants at the campfire
- Pause single building independently
- Customize refunds when demolishing buildings
- Preserve contents of granaries, stockpiles, and armories when demolished
- Apply separate goods-production multipliers to **human or AI** players or convert additional produced goods into extra gold to eg. boost the economy of AI
- Set global multipliers for market buying and selling prices or configure individual multipliers for every good (only human or everyone)
- Prevent AI from pausing its production buildings, emergency demolition buildings and deleting their hovels
- Customize the active duration of plague clouds and how far apothecaries search for them
- Multiply Lords HP
- Gates time settings and mark gates as "never auto close/open"
