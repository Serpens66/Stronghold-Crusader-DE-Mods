ExtraFeatures.KillReward.Gold.Help=Gold awarded to the victorious player as a percentage of the defeated player's gold, rounded down. -1 uses Vanilla: 25%, at least 500 gold. Values from 0 to 100 use exactly that percentage without a minimum; 0 gives no gold. The gold limit applies afterwards (Vanilla: 100,000).
ExtraFeatures.KillReward.Goods.Help=Goods awarded to the victorious player as a percentage of each eligible stock of the defeated player, rounded down separately and delivered as incoming goods. -1 uses Vanilla: 25% per goods type, without a separate loot limit. 0 gives no goods.
ExtraFeatures.KillReward.Maximum.Help=Maximum gold awarded per defeated Lord. -1 keeps the Vanilla limit of 100,000 gold. Any positive whole number sets the absolute limit, independently of the gold percentage. A limit below 500 also overrides the Vanilla minimum. This setting applies to the victorious player.
